AW16BMST2 Bench Morticer
-------------------

This morticer is essentially similar in function to a pillar drill, there's a large press handle for moving a chisel and bit up and down, to enable cutting square and rectangular slots in your workpiece (known as mortices in carpentry and joinery), with an adjustable vice on 2 axes, similar to a milling machine, to enable accurate cutting of longer or wider slots than the size of the chisel will allow.
The large wheel controls the transverse axis (left to right, or x), the smaller wheel controls the lateral axis (fron to back, or y).
